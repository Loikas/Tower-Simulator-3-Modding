# Aircraft Effects

Adds touchdown smoke bursts, wet-weather wheel spray and airport ambience. Visual and sound features can be configured separately.

Smoke starts with a burst and tapers quickly. Particles fade with their own age rather than fading an entire existing column. Wet spray has a separate appearance and depends on weather and aircraft state.

Sounds include recorded jet loops, adapted APU ambience, ground-vehicle ambience, wind and distant thunder. Keep the sound credits supplied with the mod when redistributing an allowed package. The sounds do not create a new airport service simulation.

If effects are missing, test an aircraft that lands after the mod is running. A parked aircraft is not a touchdown test. For magenta materials, confirm the installed version and capture the aircraft/model/weather details. The current suite uses repaired wheel emitters and compatible materials.

For performance comparisons, disable one optional feature at a time and keep the same save/view. Developer mode is not needed for effects themselves. See [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md).
