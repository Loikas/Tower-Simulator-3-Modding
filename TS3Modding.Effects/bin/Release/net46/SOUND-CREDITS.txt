Preparation for all files: publicly available audio, excerpting, mono mixdown,
low-pass/resampling to 22050 Hz PCM16, DC removal, peak normalization and
loop crossfade or short edge fades. Source pages and hashes retained in local
validation records. No endorsement by the artists is implied.
Game audio is used only from the user's installation and is not redistributed.
